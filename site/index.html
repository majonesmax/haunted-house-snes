Haunted House: By Matchlight

Atari 2600 Haunted House, rule for rule, as a native Super Nintendo game - in a mansion with a cellar and four floors, by the light of a match. Happy Halloween

Keys

Enterstart a game (any key, past the story)
Shiftchoose the game, 1-9
Za hint (on the title: the help)
X+Shiftlights on (a cheat)

How to play

You're the pair of eyes, in the dark. Find the three pieces of the urn and carry the whole urn out of the front door, on the Foyer's right-hand edge, where you start.

Light a match to see what's near you. A monster in the room blows it out. You can hold one thing at a time: the scepter keeps monsters from chasing you; the master key opens locked doors.

Game 1 is the gentlest: the walls always show. Stairs are at the rooms' edges.

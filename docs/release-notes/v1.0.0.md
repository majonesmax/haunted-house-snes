The first release of *Haunted House: By Matchlight* for the Super Nintendo: Atari 2600 *Haunted House*, rule
for rule, as a native SNES game, in the NES game's mansion - the Cellar, the Foyer, the Gallery, the Bedrooms
and the Attic - drawn in the remaster's SNES look and played on its orchestra.

- **Download** `haunted_house.sfc` and open it in any SNES emulator (a 512K LoROM cartridge, FastROM).
  `SHA256SUMS.txt` has its checksum.
- **Controls:** the D-pad walks; B or A lights a match and puts down what you hold; Start starts a game;
  Select chooses the game (1-9); X gives a hint in play and the help on the title; B + Select is the
  lights-on cheat.
- **The same game as the original:** the engine is checked frame by frame against the remaster's model of
  the original, on this mansion and on the original's - every frame exact.
- **What the SNES adds:** a match's light that fades at its edge, lightning that washes the walls, a storm
  over the title, the screen warming to the light outside as you escape, and an orchestra of samples for the
  songs and the twenty sound effects.
- **Nothing from the 2600 game** is in the repository or in the ROM. To play the original's own mansion,
  build it yourself from your copy:
  [docs/faithful-build.md](https://github.com/majonesmax/haunted-house-snes/blob/main/docs/faithful-build.md)
  has the recipe.

One change to *Haunted House: By Matchlight* for the Super Nintendo, from a player's notes on the NES game.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` has the checksum.

- **The scepter is a golden staff.** Its head had a gold piece hanging off one side, and it read as an axe.
  Now it's a gold point, a red jewelled orb and a collar on a straight staff, as in the NES game. What
  touches it is what you see, so its outline for picking it up follows the new picture.

The browser page plays this ROM: https://majonesmax.github.io/haunted-house-snes/
